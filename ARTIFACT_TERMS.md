# OCK Rotor Free — Artifact Terms

OCK Rotor Free is distributed as an experimental Linux binary artifact.

Permitted public uses include personal, educational, research and
experimental evaluation.

This binary distribution does not grant public access to the private
Rust implementation.

Commercial organizational use, source access, integration, technical
transfer and support are handled separately by OKCompressor / OKC.

OCK Rotor is experimental cryptographic software. It is not audited
encryption. Keep independent backups of important data.
